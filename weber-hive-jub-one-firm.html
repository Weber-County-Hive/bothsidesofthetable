One Firm, Every Seat

J-U-B Engineers writes Ogden Valley City's founding land-use plan. It also serves as the city's own engineer. Nine other government and quasi-government bodies in the same small area hire the same firm — including the two ski resorts a boundary committee kept out of the city altogether.

How to read this

  1. J-U-B Engineers Inc. is a private, for-profit consulting firm — not a government agency.
  2. Ogden Valley City, incorporated in January 2026, has no in-house planning or engineering department. It hired J-U-B for both.
  3. Below, every confirmed client relationship is sourced to a public record — the city's own website, JUB's own project pages, or public bid postings. No records request was needed for any of it.

Two roles, one city, no separation

Planner

Brian Carver

Planning Manager, J-U-B Engineers. Writes Ogden Valley City's general plan, transportation master plan, and zoning roadmap — the document that sets what gets built where.

Deputy City Engineer

Matthew Crump, P.E.

A separate J-U-B employee, serving as the city's own engineering authority — the role that checks whether what gets built meets standards.

Confirmed directly on the city's own website: "Ogden Valley City has contracted with J-U-B Engineers, Inc. to provide a broad spectrum of engineering and community planning services." Council-level oversight of the engineering role is provided by a single councilmember, not an independent engineering department. For a city with no staff of its own, the firm that plans what should be built and the firm that certifies it was built right are the same firm.

Where the threads connect

THE BOUNDARY

JUB serves Powder Mountain — the resort the city excluded

Powder Mountain was deliberately kept outside Ogden Valley City's incorporation boundary by a committee that included the resort's current president. JUB does direct infrastructure work for the resort itself — a water line project (2025) and sewer lagoon maintenance (2023), both posted under "Powder Mountain Water" and "Powder Mountain Sewer." The same firm sits on both sides of the line that boundary drew.

THE TRACK RECORD

JUB has a history of winning regional grant money for clients

Per JUB's own marketing material, the firm "helped obtain nearly $24 million dollars from the Wasatch Front Regional Council Surface Transportation Program" for a different client city, Clinton City. Ogden Valley City's own planning work is funded through a similar WFRC program (Transportation and Land Use Connection).
